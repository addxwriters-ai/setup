## 3. Services

### 3.1 Airport Transfers
Premium airport transfer service where comfort, safety, and punctuality are top priorities; professional chauffeurs dedicated to smooth journeys to and from the airport. Serves Newark Liberty (EWR) and the wider NJ/NY area.

### 3.2 Chauffeur Service
Flexible packages from hourly bookings to full-day service, tailored to the client's schedule and preferences. Chauffeurs are trained professionals prioritizing comfort, safety, and privacy — suited to high-profile events and discreet transport alike.

### 3.3 Hourly Hire
Perfect for business or leisure — meetings, errands, or a day out — with a vehicle and chauffeur retained by the hour.

### 3.4 Limousine Service
Available for weddings, prom, corporate events, and nights out on the town.

### 3.5 City to City Rides
Long-distance point-to-point transfers between cities across the served region.
